Totality

Totality is an eclipse-themed marble shooter — Zuma on steroids. A chain of glyph-orbs rolls along orbital grooves toward the Core; firing from the Eclipse Idol, you match three or more to pop the chain, trigger magnetic snap-back combos, and fill the Corona Gauge before the chain arrives. It's a real campaign, not an endless loop: 25 levels across the four Houses of the Sun — Dawn, Zenith, Ember, Night — ending in a finale called TOTALITY, with Endless Orbit unlocking after. The name is the ending: the finale isn't stopped, it's survived — you win by letting totality happen. Built by Solstice Interactive; the eclipse is the Solstice mark.
